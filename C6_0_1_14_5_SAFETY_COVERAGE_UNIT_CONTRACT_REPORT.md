## Required questions (Q1-Q26)

**Q1. Did C6.0.1.14.4 utility depend on Atom/Child count or segmentation?**  
Yes. Correlations were 0.9338323297 with Atom count and 0.6571135475 with Child count, and the same-core legacy synthetic utility changed by 9,000,000. C6.0.1.14.5 removes all three count features from Unit utility.

**Q2. Are ID and utility identical for 1, 3, and 10 Children over the same union?**  
Yes. Unit ID, Unit utility vector, and planner contribution are identical in all three probe partitions.

**Q3. What are the responsibilities of Unit, Atom, Child, and Bundle?**  
The Unit is the stable value/coverage identity. Atoms provide source-native evidence geometry. Children are alternative segmentation pieces. Bundles are selectable delivery groups. Children and Bundles realize Units but do not multiply their value.

**Q4. How many Units and tiers?**  
81 Units: Tier 5 = 73, Tier 4 = 5, Tier 3 = 3, all lower tiers = 0.

**Q5. Why not add Atom/Child tiers?**  
Because partitioning is an implementation choice. Summing pieces rewards arbitrary fragmentation and changes utility without changing the underlying speech evidence.

**Q6. How do the two contracts differ?**  
Target-Core asks whether a localized machine target covers at least 0.80 of an error while covering at least 0.95 of its Unit. Anchored Playback additionally requires a same-Unit machine anchor, physical playable audio, at least 0.95 Unit playback coverage, at least 0.80 error playback coverage, bounded envelope/context, and full provenance.

**Q7. Why is Anchored Playback the product contract?**  
Human-on-exception safety depends on a reviewer hearing machine-justified evidence. Target localization alone cannot prove that the delivered audio is linked to the same Unit or is actually reviewable.

**Q8. How is accidental adjacent overlap rejected?**  
Playback must reference a selected eligible realization for the same Unit, meet the 0.20 s/0.10 machine-anchor gate, bind hashed physical audio and playback transforms, and pass both Unit and error coverage thresholds. Bare geometric overlap cannot pass.

**Q9. What machine-anchor minimums apply?**  
At least 0.20 seconds and at least 0.10 of the Unit.

**Q10. What Review Audio coverage applies to Unit Core and the evaluated error?**  
At least 0.95 of the Unit Core and at least 0.80 of the evaluated error.

**Q11. How many realization candidates exist?**  
346 eligible core-complete and 836 eligible anchored-playback realizations, 1,182 eligible total.

**Q12. Can Children be individually feasible while the full Unit is jointly infeasible?**  
Yes. Seven contract rows per contract are proven infeasible under joint budgets even though historical child-level exchangeability exists; individual feasibility is therefore insufficient.

**Q13. Can Core be infeasible while Playback is feasible?**  
Yes. This run has exactly one Unit whose Core contract is `INFEASIBLE_PROVEN` while its Playback contract is `JOINTLY_FEASIBLE`. Both contracts have 7 infeasible rows overall, but their candidate sets and constraints differ, so one status is never inferred from the other.

**Q14. Why does the old 32/32-style individual result not prove safety?**  
It proves only one-at-a-time substitutions. It does not prove simultaneous Bundle selection, complete Unit core, bounded audio, Phase-A preservation, or the 24/60/95% constraints.

**Q15. Was the old `forcedFeasibleUnselected.length === 0` completion gate removed?**  
Yes. The route artifact records `forcedFeasibleUnselectedGateRemoved=true`.

**Q16. Is an individually feasible unselected Child a blocker?**  
Diagnostic only. The blocker count is 0; the diagnostic count is 32.

**Q17. Is the Unit-aware search complete and certified?**  
Yes. The exact deterministic lexicographic planner is complete, feasible, non-degrading, and has a frozen certificate with 11/11 hard constraints passing.

**Q18. Are Phase A obligations fully preserved?**  
Yes: coverage 1.0, dropped obligations 0, regressions 0.

4/6 (0.6666666667).

**Q22. How much independent Review Audio was added, and is the queue below 95%?**

**Q23. Why could product 6/6 with Target Core below 6/6 enter C6.0.1.15 but not authorize machine-only release?**  
The frozen product contract measures human-on-exception audibility; its success would justify ranker recalibration for review delivery. It does not establish correct machine subtitle text/timing. The incomplete Target-Core metric remains an advisory, and `machineOnlyReleaseAuthorized` remains false.

**Q24. Was `GO_C6_0_1_15_REGION_RANKER_RECALIBRATION` reached?**

**Q25. Which failure class applies?**

Segmentation, search completeness, contract freeze, Review Audio delivery, and evaluation integrity all passed.

**Q26. Why would even 6/6 not directly start a human trial or C6.0.2?**
