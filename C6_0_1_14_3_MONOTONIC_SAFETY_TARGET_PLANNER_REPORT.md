## Acceptance conclusion

**Machine planning and delivery engineering passed; Gold evaluation integrity failed.** The authoritative route is therefore `HOLD_FOR_EVALUATION_INTEGRITY_REPAIR`, not `GO_C6_0_1_15_REGION_RANKER_RECALIBRATION`.

The replay-only machine route preserved all 24 Phase A obligations and all 2,640,993 native core samples. The deterministic branch-and-bound planner found a verified complete witness with 24 bundles, 41 children, exactly 60 seconds of speech core, 837.900681 seconds of unique full-queue audio (93.0990% of the episode), zero whole-episode catch-all targets, and zero canonical text/timing/presentation mutations. All 84 physical WAVs were generated, playable, and byte-hash verified, and all 333 targets mapped to physical audio and playback.

After both Final A and Final B were frozen, the isolated Gold evaluator correctly failed closed: all 84 C6.0.1.14.3 audio projection rows carried stale row-level semantic hashes after the strict projection removed `nativeSourceInterval`. The WAV hashes themselves still pass, but A4–A7 cannot be used to claim Missing or Hallucination recall. A0–A3 remain valid: A0 4/6, A1 5/6, A2 4/6, A3 5/6 Missing audible, and 1/1 Hallucination audible. The apparent A4–A7 `0/6` and `0/1` values are integrity-failure sentinels, not measured recall. Since Gold had already been read, source, tests, schemas, selection, and projection code were not changed afterward.

Production remains `needs-review`, `finalizationMethod: none`; C6.0.2 remains `HOLD`; no real human trial was started.
