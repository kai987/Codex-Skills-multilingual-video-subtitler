## Required answers Q1–Q22

**Q1. Legacy nonzero counts?** Candidate `0/972`, Atom `0/1390`, eligible Child `0/135`, eligible Bundle `0/336`; selected bundle legacy-priority sum `0`.

**Q2. Earliest root cause?** The earliest causes were absent text-candidate priority sources and, more importantly, speech evidence never entering legacy priority. Candidate→Atom association loss was not the primary cause: 1,242 atoms had associations yet all remained zero.

**Q3. Why may text utility zero not zero speech utility?** Text availability measures whether a reviewer has an edit-start candidate; detector evidence measures whether speech should be heard. Credible speech can exist when every ASR text candidate is absent or unsafe.

**Q4. How are the three utilities separated?** `speechEvidenceUtility` ranks verified speech activity; `textCandidateUtility` ranks candidate usefulness without reading raw text; `reviewSafetyUtility` is the speech-led, budget-facing lexicographic vector with text as a lower-order term.

**Q5. How are multiple views/profiles deduplicated?** They are normalized to one detector/correlation family before utility aggregation. Silero multi-view/profile, Pyannote standard/exclusive from one inference, and WebRTC multi-view each contribute one family vote.

**Q6. Did every positive atom receive nonzero speech utility?** Yes, `1375/1375`; zero-after count `0`.

**Q7. Are any eligible children or bundles still all-zero?** No: children `135/135` nonzero and eligible bundles `336/336` nonzero; both zero-after counts are `0`.

**Q8. Is there silent utility loss?** No. Evidence→Atom, Atom→Child, Child→Bundle, and Bundle→Planner silent drops are all `0`; two geometry drops are explicit and reasoned.

**Q9. Why not maximize raw speech samples first?** The 60-second core budget saturates, and raw length lets ordinary long intervals dominate shorter independently supported speech while providing no safety distinction.

**Q10. How does the objective prevent that?** T5/T4/T3 ordinal utility precedes capped duration; family/stratum breadth and diversity precede low-tier coverage; per-child duration is bucketed and capped.

**Q11. Is the old plan feasible under the new policy?** Yes; the witness verified under unchanged hard constraints.

**Q12. Is the new utility objective nondegraded?** Yes; lexicographic comparison of the recorded vectors passed.
